Curriculum vitae

I am a professor in the Department of Markets, Public Policy & Law at Boston University, an editor of the Journal of Health Economics, and a Research Associate at the National Bureau of Economic Research. My research focuses on health insurance, health care markets, and household finance. I received my Ph.D. from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology and my bachelor’s degree from the University of Chicago. Before joining Boston University, I taught at the University of Miami and Columbia University. You can find my curriculum vitae here.

Textbook

“Better Health Economics: An Introduction for Everyone,” with Matthew Notowidigdo. A textbook published by University of Chicago Press. Available from the University of Chicago Press and Amazon.
